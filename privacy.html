Toll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

Toll is built to be private. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and almost everything the app records stays on your device. Only two things can ever leave it, and both are optional and controlled by a parent: the progress summary email, and anonymous learning stats used for comparisons and daily competitions. We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party tracking.

Who this applies to

Toll is an iOS app, set up and managed by a parent or guardian, that asks a quiz question before opening selected apps — turning screen time into learning time. This policy covers the Toll iOS app and its supporting services.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else, except as part of the two optional features described below:

  • Quiz history, scores, streaks, mastery progress, and test results
  • Your learning groups — the subjects you study and the apps each one gates
  • App settings, theme, and the parent's PIN
  • Your consent choices, and the age-check result (a yes/no plus a short label — never a birthdate)

Deleting the app removes all of this.

Screen Time and app locking

Toll uses Apple's Screen Time technology (Family Controls and Device Activity) to notice when a gated app is opened and to apply or lift the quiz lock. This runs entirely on your device through Apple's framework. Toll never sees or transmits which apps you use beyond what you explicitly configure, and the list of gated apps stays on the device.

Age check

To confirm the app is being set up appropriately, Toll uses Apple's Declared Age Range feature. Apple returns only an age range (for example, "16 or older") — never a date of birth. Toll stores only whether the check passed and a coarse age band, on your device. Nothing about your age is sent to us beyond that band (see the anonymous stats section below).

Progress summary email (optional)

A parent can turn on a daily or weekly summary email in Settings. When it's on, the app sends a learning summary to the email address(es) the parent enters — the parent's address and, optionally, the learner's own address. The summary includes the learner's first name (as entered in the app, to say whose stats these are), questions answered, accuracy, subjects and levels progressed, tests passed, unlock counts, and time spent in the gated apps (including those apps' names).

To deliver it, this summary passes through our backend (Supabase) and is sent from a dedicated Gmail account. It is used only to send that email — we don't keep the stats after the email is sent — and you can turn it off any time in Settings, which stops all future emails.

Anonymous comparisons and competitions (optional, parent consent)

With a parent's consent, Toll can show how a learner's progress compares with other learners their age, and let them join daily competitions (score-based challenges). To power this, the app submits learning stats — such as questions answered, accuracy, questions mastered, unlock counts, gated screen-time minutes, and daily competition scores — to our backend under a random anonymous ID created on the device, together with a coarse age band ("under 16" / "16 or older").

  • No name, nickname, email, or account is attached — there is nothing to link the ID to a person.
  • There is no public leaderboard or profile. The backend only ever returns aggregates (population counts, top scores, percentiles) — it is built so that no individual's data can be read back, by us or anyone else.
  • Consent can be withdrawn any time in Settings, which stops all submissions. You can also ask us to delete the data stored under your device's anonymous ID using the contact below.

Downloaded content

Some quiz content, such as the daily news quiz, is downloaded from our backend. This is a download only — no personal data or usage data is sent with it.

Children's privacy

Toll is parent-administered: setup requires a parent, an age check gates access, and a parent decides whether the optional email or anonymous stats are enabled. We collect no more from children than described above, we do not show advertising or use advertising or analytics trackers, and we never use children's data for marketing.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing history.
  • We do not require an account, and we never see your Apple ID.

Service providers

  • Apple — provides the on-device Screen Time and age-range features.
  • Supabase — hosts the backend used for the summary email, anonymous stats, and downloadable quiz content. Like any internet service, it processes standard connection data (such as IP addresses) to serve requests; we never link this to learning stats.
  • Google (Gmail) — delivers the summary email.

These providers act on our behalf to run the app and may not use the data for their own purposes.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On-device data: deleted when you delete the app.
  • Summary email: turn it off any time in Settings; we don't retain the stats after each email is sent.
  • Anonymous stats: withdraw consent in Settings to stop submissions, or contact us to delete the data stored under your anonymous ID.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data, and withdraw consent, at any time. Most of this you can do directly in the app; for anything else — including deletion requests — contact us at the address below.
