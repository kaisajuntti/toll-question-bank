Toll — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

Toll is built to be private. Almost everything the app records stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. The only data that leaves your device is the weekly summary email you choose to turn on, and — if you opt in — a made-up nickname and quiz scores for competitions. We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party tracking.

Who this applies to

Toll is set up and managed by a parent or guardian and used to help with learning and screen-time habits. This policy covers the Toll iOS app.

What stays on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • Quiz history, scores, and learning progress (questions mastered, tests passed)
  • Your learning subjects and the list of apps you choose to gate
  • App settings, theme, and your PIN
  • Your consent choices, and whether the age check passed (a yes/no plus a short label — never a birthdate)

Deleting the app removes all of this.

Age check

To confirm the app is being set up appropriately, Toll uses Apple's Declared Age Range feature. Apple returns only an age range (for example, "16 or older") — never a date of birth. Toll stores only whether the check passed, on your device. Nothing about your age is sent to us.

Weekly summary email (optional)

If a parent turns on the summary email, Toll sends a learning summary (such as questions answered, screen-time totals, and per-subject progress) to the email address(es) the parent enters. To deliver it, this information passes through our backend provider (Supabase) and an email delivery service (Google/Gmail). It is used only to send that email — nothing else — and you can turn it off any time in Settings, which stops all future emails.

Competitions (optional, opt-in)

If a parent allows competitions, Toll stores a made-up nickname (never a real name) together with quiz scores on our backend (Supabase) so players can appear on a leaderboard. No real names, contacts, or other personal details are collected for this. You can turn competitions off any time in Settings → Competitions, and you can ask us to delete a nickname and its scores using the contact below.

Children's privacy

Toll is parent-administered: setup requires a parent, an age check gates access, and a parent provides consent before any optional data (the email or competitions) is collected. We do not show advertising, use advertising or analytics trackers, or collect location or contacts.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or browsing history.

Service providers

  • Apple — provides the on-device Screen Time and age-range features.
  • Supabase — hosts the backend used for the summary email and competitions.
  • Google (Gmail) — delivers the summary email.

Keeping and deleting data

  • On-device data: deleted when you delete the app.
  • Summary email: stop it any time in Settings; we don't retain the stats after the email is sent.
  • Competitions: turn it off in Settings, or contact us to delete a nickname and its scores.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data, and withdraw consent at any time. For anything in this policy, or to request deletion, contact us at the address below.
